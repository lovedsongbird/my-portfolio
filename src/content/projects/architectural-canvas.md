## Overview

This project explored the relationship between physical space, architectural scale, and organic textures. Hand-built and stretched on custom frames, the final installation spans across the main atrium.

## Process & Materials

* Hand-built pine stretchers reinforced for tensile load.
* Heavy unprimed cotton duck canvas.
* Layered mineral pigments, gesso, and raw fluid acrylics.
